# Superstore Profit Analysis Dashboard (Tableau)

## Project Overview
In this Tableau project, we investigate profitability issues across product categories in Superstore’s 2015 performance. The CEO has flagged certain inventory segments as underperforming, and this analysis was conducted to identify consistent loss-makers, geographic trends, and potential business drivers behind the profit decline.

## Business Context
As of January 2016, Superstore leadership seeks to improve business outcomes for the year. The finance team has observed recurring profit concerns across specific products. This analysis is aimed at answering:

- Which products or categories experienced profit loss for multiple years?
- Where (geographically) are these losses occurring?
- What could be driving these underperformances?

## Objectives
- Identify SKUs or categories with long-standing profitability issues
- Compare net profit and year-over-year profit changes
- Understand regional or segment-level contributions to profit loss
- Provide actionable insights through interactive Tableau dashboards

## Tools & Technologies
- **Tableau Desktop**: Visual analytics & dashboard creation
- **Superstore Dataset**: Sample retail sales data
- **Calculated Fields**: Profit YoY delta, Profit Ratio
- **Filters & Parameters**: Region, Category, Year toggles for stakeholder views

## Key Dashboards
-  **Profitability Trends by Category & Sub-Category**
-  **Geographic Breakdown of Profit Performance**
-  **Product-Level Profit Change (YoY)**
-  **Executive Summary: KPIs & Recommendations**

## Stakeholders
This dashboard is designed to be intuitive and accessible for:
- Leadership and Executives
- Sales and Regional Managers
- Finance and Accounting Teams
- Marketing Analysts
- Supply Chain & Manufacturing

## Outcomes
- Identified top loss-making products and regions
- Visualized year-over-year profit change to flag repeat underperformers
- Empowered strategic decision-making with targeted, interactive visuals
