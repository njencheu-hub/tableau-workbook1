## Project Overview
In this Tableau project, we investigate profitability issues across product categories in Superstore’s 2015 performance. The CEO has flagged certain inventory segments as underperforming, and this analysis was conducted to identify consistent loss-makers, geographic trends, and potential business drivers behind the profit decline.

---

## Business Context
In early 2016, Superstore’s executive team raised concerns about declining profits in several inventory segments. The finance team requested a deep-dive analysis to answer:

- Which product categories consistently underperform?
- Are losses concentrated in specific regions?
- What patterns or drivers are contributing to profit decline?

---

## Objectives
- Flag SKUs and categories with recurring profitability issues
- Analyze year-over-year profit trends and net margins
- Map geographic loss clusters by state or region
- Build a Tableau dashboard to inform strategic decisions

---

## Real-World Use Cases

- **Executive Strategy**: Support decisions on discontinuing or repricing low-margin products
- **Regional Targeting**: Identify regions that need supply chain, marketing, or sales intervention
- **Product Lifecycle**: Evaluate category performance over time to guide future inventory planning
- **Cross-Team Collaboration**: Enable alignment between finance, sales, and operations using shared KPIs

---

## Tools & Technologies
- **Tableau Desktop** – for building interactive dashboards and visuals
- **Superstore Dataset** – retail sales sample including Order, Profit, Category, and Region data
- **Calculated Fields** – e.g., Profit Year-over-Year Delta, Profit Ratio
- **Dynamic Filters & Parameters** – for category, region, and time-based exploration

---

## Key Dashboards
- **Profitability by Category & Sub-Category**  
  Visualize top and bottom performers over time.

- **Geographic Profit Breakdown**  
  Identify loss-prone states or regions.

- **Product-Level YoY Profit Change**  
  Track product-level deltas and trends.

- **Executive Summary Dashboard**  
  Highlights KPIs, strategic takeaways, and next steps.

---

## Stakeholders
This dashboard is designed to be intuitive and accessible for:
- Leadership and Executives
- Sales and Regional Managers
- Finance and Accounting Teams
- Marketing Analysts
- Supply Chain & Manufacturing

## Outcomes
- Revealed the top 10 loss-making SKUs and the regions contributing most to net losses
- Detected product categories with declining YoY profitability
- Empowered leadership with actionable insights for 2016 strategy

## Installation

1. Ensure you have **Tableau Desktop** installed  
2. Download the `.twb` file from the repository  
3. Open it in Tableau Desktop

---

## Usage

- Navigate through the **dashboard tabs**
- Use filters for `Region`, `Category`, and `Year` to explore trends
- Review the **Executive Summary** for high-level KPIs and recommendations

PowerPoint version of the insights is available in `super_store1.pptx`.

_Note: Tableau workbook (`.twb`) file is included in the repository.
